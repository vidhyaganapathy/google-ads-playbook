# Google Ads Playbook

**Search, Performance Max and Demand Gen: campaign structuring, asset groups and conversion goals.**

Search captures intent, Demand Gen creates it, and Performance Max scales across channels, all optimising to the same conversion goals.

## The campaign mix

| Campaign | Job | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **Search** | Capture people already looking | Always the foundation |
| **Performance Max** | Scale across Google's channels with automation | Conversion tracking is solid and there's enough volume to learn |
| **Demand Gen** | Create demand with visual ads | You need more people searching for you, or have strong creative |

---

## Principles

- **Conversions before campaigns.** Automation only optimises toward what you measure.
- **Structure by intent, not by keyword list.** Group by what the searcher wants.
- **Negatives are half the job.** Review search terms every week.
- **Give automation guardrails.** Brand exclusions, good signals and clean conversion goals.
