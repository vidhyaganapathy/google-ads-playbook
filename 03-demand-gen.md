# 03 · Demand Gen

Demand Gen runs image and video ads across Google's more visual surfaces, such as YouTube (including Shorts), Discover and Gmail. Use it to **create demand**: reach people before they're searching, so Search has more to capture later.

## When to use it

- Search volume for your category is low or saturated
- You have strong creative (especially video and creator content)
- You want to retarget site visitors and viewers with visual ads
- You want to test messaging before scaling it elsewhere

## Structure

```
Campaign: DemandGen_[Objective]_[Market]
├── Ad group: Lookalike of converters
├── Ad group: Custom segment (people searching related terms)
└── Ad group: Retargeting (site visitors, YouTube viewers)
```

## Creative

| Format | Tips |
|---|---|
| **Video** | Hook in the first seconds; include vertical for Shorts; captions on |
| **Image** | Multiple ratios; clear product and benefit; minimal text |
| **Carousel** | Step-by-step or feature-by-feature stories |
| **Creator video** | Strong for trust; see whitelisting-playbook for rights and briefs |

## Audiences

- **Lookalike segments** built from your converters or customer lists
- **Custom segments** based on search terms, websites or apps your buyers use
- **Your data** for retargeting

## How I judge it

Demand Gen is upper funnel, so judge it on more than last-click:
- Cost per conversion (and cost per qualified lead where tracked)
- **Lift in branded search** and direct traffic over time
- View-through and engaged-view conversions, read with care
- Performance of retargeting audiences it builds
